posix-lite — subset POSIX para o port MC (e outros apps)

Fonte: posix.c
Saida:  c_compiler/out/POSIX.DLL  (make -C c_compiler out/POSIX.DLL)

Headers no cc:
  include/unistd.h, dirent.h, fcntl.h, sys/types.h, sys/stat.h

Kernel: k_pwd, k_chdir, k_dirlist

Uso (cc):
  CC APP.C POSIX.DLL

Apps gcc -mx32: recompilar posix.c com XGFLAGS e linkar com LIBCX.DLL
(ainda nao ha POSIXX.DLL separado; proximo passo se o MC precisar).
