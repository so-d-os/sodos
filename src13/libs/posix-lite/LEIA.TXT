posix-lite — subset POSIX para o port MC (e outros apps)

Fonte: posix.c
Saida:  c_compiler/out/POSIX.DLL  (cc)  e  out/POSIXX.DLL (gcc -mx32)

Headers no cc:
  include/unistd.h, dirent.h, fcntl.h, sys/types.h, sys/stat.h

O que tem:
  open/close/read/write/lseek/creat (arquivo inteiro na memoria, ate 64 MB;
  grava no close ou no fsync), O_CREAT/O_TRUNC/O_APPEND/O_EXCL, ftruncate,
  dup, fstat/stat (com st_mtime da entrada FAT), access, unlink, mkdir,
  rmdir, chdir, getcwd, isatty, opendir/readdir/closedir, getpid & cia,
  sleep/usleep (espera ocupada no k_ms), sysconf. read(0) le do stdin.

Na libc (LIBC.DLL), nao aqui: time.h, sys/time.h, setjmp.h, signal.h,
  locale.h, math.h, float.h, inttypes.h, scanf, rename/remove/tmpnam,
  getenv/setenv (ambiente so do processo), realpath.

Kernel: k_pwd, k_chdir, k_dirlist, k_dir, k_fdelete, k_mkdir, k_rmdir

Uso (cc):
  CC APP.C POSIX.DLL
